Real combat stats · Attack & Special: HP damage (bars /50) · Defend: % of next hit blocked (bar /100) · Heal: HP restored (bar /20). 100 HP per fighter. Special recharges after 3 own turns.

Original articulated 3D fighters. Roster tiles are portraits; the selected preview and battle play skeletal animation. Static portraits appear if 3D is unavailable.

Make your move.

Take your fighter from 100 HP to victory. Reduce your opponent to zero to win.

Attack
Deal your fighter’s Attack stat in HP damage.
Defend
Block your fighter’s Defend percentage of the next hit. Shield does not stack; even the strongest guard takes damage.
Heal
Restore your fighter’s Heal stat, up to the 100 HP maximum.
Special
Deal your fighter’s Special stat in HP damage, then wait three of your own turns to recharge.

Play your way

Friend: a real online 1v1. You are Player 1; invite one friend as Player 2. The host chooses both fighters and starts the match. Each player controls only their own moves. Choices resolve immediately; a missed 15-second turn is chosen randomly.

Bot: choose your move to resolve it immediately. The bot takes its turn after one second.

Groups: create a room and share its link or QR code. Friends join balanced teams and vote on their phones. The presenter starts when both teams have players. Votes can change during the 15-second turn; ties and empty votes choose at random.
