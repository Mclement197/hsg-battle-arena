# Campus arcade surface
Mode: Experience, with Operate constraints on match controls.
Scope: /game/, all five primary screens and multiplayer supporting screens.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Turn HSG archetypes into an arcade fight roster, not a recolored classroom dashboard.
OWN-WORLD: Forest-black grounds, emerald-lit angular frames, white Barlow Condensed italic display, compact Barlow labels and segmented command meters. All material remains campus-themed.
STORY: Choose one of three distinct modes, a campus arena, two profiled fighters, then defeat the opponent through server-authoritative moves. The presenter alone runs Groups; a Friend host actually fights.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Home places the user-supplied HSG Learning Center photograph behind a background-only translucent green overlay, keeping the stepped glass facade recognizable on desktop and phone. Foreground stays fully opaque. Home has an oversized three-line title and three stacked mode selectors opposite two large fighter cutouts. Selection gives the left third to the active fighter and the remaining two thirds to six portrait cards, each with four labeled numeric segmented bars. Mobile places a compact active preview above a two-column roster.
FORM: User-pinned arcade selection reference, translated from red to HSG green; no random alternate world overrides it. Code-led implementation uses the existing replaceable original vector assets. Seed: none (user-pinned direction).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: switching the fighter changes a large preview and four authoritative stat bars, with a short brightness-lock cue. Under reduced motion, the swap is immediate. Combat action effects remain resolution-only, never a preview of local votes.

Open decisions: final production fighter/arena artwork can replace placeholder SVGs by the existing ID filenames. No inferred commercial or licensing claims.
