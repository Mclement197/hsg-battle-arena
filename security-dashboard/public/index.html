How the assessment works

Rubric weighs four security categories and two quality indicators. Missing or failed sources contribute zero. Confidence is the share of rubric weight backed by measurements.

See score breakdown

Measured security categories start at 100 and lose 40 per critical, 20 per high, 8 per medium and 2 per low active finding, floored at zero. Accepted risks still count. Evidence older than 48 hours halves the score; after seven days it scores zero.
